Civil-Military Operations

Civil-Military Operations are an important part of people-centered engagement and community partnership.

Through coordination with government agencies, local communities, organizations, and other stakeholders, CMO activities can contribute to peace, development, resilience, and public service.

04 / HISTORY

CMO Through the Years

1954

Civic Action

Philippine Marines undertook civic action projects in Cotabato, including assistance related to community needs and school construction.

1984

Civil-Military Operations

Official PMC history records the beginning of a CMO-oriented approach by the 3rd Marine Brigade.

2002

8th Marine Battalion

The official PMC history records the 8th Marine Battalion as Best Marine Battalion of 2002 for numerous CMO activities in Southern Philippines.

TODAY

People-Centered Engagement

CMO continues to emphasize partnership, community relations, humanitarian assistance, and support to peace and development.
