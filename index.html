01 / ABOUT CMO

People-Centered Engagement

Civil-Military Operations support communication, cooperation, coordination, and meaningful engagement between military organizations, government partners, stakeholders, and communities.

Through people-centered activities, CMO can help strengthen relationships, promote public awareness, support humanitarian initiatives, and contribute to community resilience and development.

Building understanding through communication, cooperation, and service.
